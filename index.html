About Me

Miwako Kogawa (she/her)

Hi, I'm Miwako — a creative project coordinator and employer branding specialist who enjoys bringing people, ideas and creativity together to create meaningful experiences. My career began in luxury retail at Louis Vuitton's Omotesando flagship and grew into HR, where I discovered that what motivates me most is the creative and strategic side of building a brand and engaging people.

I grew up in Australia, studied at Waseda University's School of International Liberal Studies, and work in both English and Japanese — happiest collaborating across cultures, improving how things are done, and seeing ideas become reality.

Case Study

The Recruitment Website

The challenge. As part of a new graduate recruitment strategy aimed at growing the proportion of younger managers in the business, Louis Vuitton needed a recruitment website that spoke to the next generation of talent — with a look and voice worthy of the brand.

My approach. I led the project independently from concept through launch: coordinating vendors, managing creative direction, selecting photography, overseeing content, and keeping every stakeholder aligned along the way.
