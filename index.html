About Me

Miwako Kogawa (she/her)

Hi, I'm Miwako, a creative project coordinator and employer branding specialist. I bring people, ideas and creativity together to create meaningful experiences. My career began in luxury retail at Louis Vuitton's Omotesando flagship and grew into Human Resources, where I discovered that what motivates me most is the creative and strategic work of building a brand and engaging people.

I grew up in Australia and studied at Waseda University's School of International Liberal Studies. I work in both English and Japanese, and I am happiest collaborating across cultures, improving how things are done, and seeing ideas become reality.
