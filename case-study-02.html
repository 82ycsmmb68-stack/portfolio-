Overview

The existing recruitment website worked as an information portal, but it did not feel like Louis Vuitton. I led its redevelopment from concept to launch, turning a functional page into a branded platform that communicates the Maison's culture, people and career opportunities.

Visit the live website →

Research & Discovery

Before any design work, I studied what university students actually expect from a graduate recruitment website.

Competitor analysis

Recruitment sites of leading employers, annotated for what worked and what did not.

Market trends

Graduate recruitment trends in Japan and the structures top employers use.

Candidate needs

The information and reassurance students look for at each stage of applying.

Journey mapping

User expectations from first visit through to submitting an application.

The insight: students wanted more than recruitment information. They wanted to understand the culture, the career progression, and the people they might work alongside.

Creative Direction

The goal: a website that immediately feels like Louis Vuitton. Working with our creative partner, I defined the visual identity around the values of the Maison.

Feedback in action

Pages from my revision notes to the design vendor: what to change, where, and why, annotated directly on the wireframes. Ten-plus rounds of this dialogue shaped the final experience.

Employee Storytelling

The most important addition: dedicated employee profile pages. Using the candidate personas, I selected three employees who joined as graduates and built careers in the Maison, then coordinated their interviews and directed the photography at the Ginza Namiki-dori flagship.

Results

2,000+ applications supported by the wider strategy, up from roughly 800
10+ rounds of design revisions to reach a brand-worthy experience
1 scalable platform that continues to support future campaigns

“Employer branding is about creating experiences, not sharing information. The website helps candidates imagine themselves as part of Louis Vuitton.”
